Bug reports people actually want to read

A bug report is a handoff. You have noticed something unexpected; another person needs enough context to see it too. Clear writing makes that handoff quick.

Make it repeatable

Start with the shortest sequence of actions that shows the issue. Include relevant setup such as the browser, test account type, or data state. Avoid combining several unrelated problems in one report.

Then state what you expected and what happened. Keep those two observations separate. A short screen recording or screenshot can help when the behavior is visual or timing-dependent.

Describe the impact

Explain who is affected and what task they cannot complete. Avoid guessing at the technical cause unless you have evidence. A neutral description helps everyone focus on understanding and fixing the problem.

Quick structure: Summary · Environment · Preconditions · Steps · Expected · Actual · Evidence · User impact.
